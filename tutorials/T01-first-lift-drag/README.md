# T01 — First trustworthy lift and drag result

This tutorial answers a deliberately narrow engineering question: can a simple
aircraft setup produce stable, reviewable lift and drag at 0° and 5° angle of
attack? The goal is not to obtain a promotional contour. The goal is to connect
intent, assumptions, Flow360 parameters, execution, and evidence without hiding
the places where engineering judgment is still required.

## Assumptions

- The supplied CSM geometry uses metres and its `groupName` faces are
  `fuselage`, `leftWing`, and `rightWing`.
- Air uses the pinned Flow360 default thermal state.
- The baseline is steady RANS at 100 m/s and 0° sideslip.
- The reference area is 24 m², reference length is 2.4 m, and moment centre is
  `[4, 0, 0] m`.
- The committed mesh sizes are tutorial fidelity. They are not evidence of mesh
  independence.

## Baseline

`simulation.json` is a complete `SimulationParams` document generated by
`build_simulation.py`. It contains:

- automatic farfield generation at 50 geometry lengths;
- 1 mm first-layer thickness and 0.5 m maximum surface edge length;
- an air Fluid model, aircraft Wall, and farfield Freestream;
- a steady 1,000-step solve with a ramped CFL from 1 to 100;
- Cp, Cf, yPlus, and CfVec surface fields;
- CL and CD histories for the complete aircraft.

The checked-in entity-info snapshot is derived from the same Flow360 Airplane
example geometry. It allows local validation to reproduce the face and ghost
surface inventory without contacting the cloud.

## Why these settings

The first-layer and surface-size settings are explicit so a reviewer can see the
assumed mesh fidelity rather than inheriting invisible defaults. Automated
farfield removes manual outer-domain construction while keeping the chosen size
visible. Reference quantities are explicit because CL, CD, and moments are
otherwise easy to compute with the wrong normalization. A ramped CFL avoids
starting immediately at the aggressive final value.

## Variant

`variants/alpha-5deg.patch.json` changes only angle of attack, including its
constructor input cache. Using a small merge patch makes the semantic difference
reviewable and preserves every other assumption. Five degrees is not assumed to
be “better”; the evidence contract requires checking force stability and the
surface response again.

## Run and evidence

Follow `plan.md` for local validation and optional cloud commands. Use
`expected/evidence.yaml` as the acceptance contract and `expected/report.md` as
the report structure. A Case is not accepted merely because Flow360 reached a
terminal success state.
